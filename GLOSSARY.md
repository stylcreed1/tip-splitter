# Tip Splitter

A page that divides the tips collected during one shift among the staff who worked it, in proportion to the hours each worked.

## Language

**Shift**:
A single work period whose tips are split. Only one Shift exists at a time.
_Avoid_: Service, session, day

**Tip pool**:
The single total amount of tips collected during a Shift, to be divided among its staff.
_Avoid_: Tips, pot, kitty, total

**Staff member**:
A person who worked the Shift and receives a Share of the Tip pool.
_Avoid_: Employee, worker, user

**Hours worked**:
The decimal number of hours a Staff member worked during the Shift.
_Avoid_: Time, duration, hours

**Share**:
The amount of the Tip pool a Staff member receives, proportional to their Hours worked. Shares are whole cents and always sum exactly to the Tip pool.
_Avoid_: Payout, cut, split
